About

I'm a 6th year Ph.D. student in the Math department at the University of Notre Dame. My advisor is Dr. Chris Schommer-Pries. I am on the job market this year.

Research

My research is mostly centered around topological quantum field theories. In particular, I'm interested in higher categories, low-dimensional topology, operadic algebras, and homotopy theory.

The focus of my current research is on using Smith ideals for $\infty$-operadic algebras to prove analogs of classical structure theorems of algebras.

Preprints

  • (WIP) Noether's Isomorphism Theorems for Smith Ideals of Operadic Algebras
    We give an $\infty$-categorical definition of Smith ideals for an $\infty$-operad in a presentably symmetric monoidal pointed $\infty$-category, then prove the four isomorphism theorems.
